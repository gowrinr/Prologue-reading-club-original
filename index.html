CHAPTER 01

ABOUT PROLOGUE

Every story
needs a beginning.

PROLOGUE is more than a reading club. It is a space where stories are discovered, voices are heard, and ideas are allowed to grow.

At LBSITW, we believe that reading is not simply about turning pages. It is about stepping into different worlds, seeing through different perspectives, and finding something of ourselves along the way.

PROLOGUE brings together students who share a curiosity for books, literature, conversations and creativity.
